Konidela Ram Charan, born 27 March 1985, is an Indian actor, dancer, producer and entrepreneur widely regarded as one of the most influential stars of Telugu cinema and Indian cinema at large.

Son of megastar Chiranjeevi and Surekha, Ram Charan made his acting debut with Chirutha (2007) and achieved his first major breakthrough with Magadheera (2009), a film that redefined Telugu action cinema for a generation. He went on to headline era-defining films such as Rangasthalam (2018), a critically acclaimed performance that remains a career milestone, before reaching a new level of global recognition through S. S. Rajamouli's RRR, alongside Jr. NTR.

Beyond acting, Ram Charan is an active film producer and entrepreneur, with ventures spanning production and business, continuing his family's long legacy in Telugu cinema while building an identity distinctly his own.
